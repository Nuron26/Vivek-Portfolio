      Idea · Learning project
      
A concept I’m exploring

      
Meet TEAMFLOW.

      
TEAMFLOW is an early project idea for a shared workspace where teams can organize tasks, coordinate projects, and keep important work in one place.

      
Team work gets scattered across messages, task lists, files, and updates. TEAMFLOW imagines bringing those pieces into a clearer workflow. It’s an idea in progress, not a finished product, and a way for me to practise product thinking, interface design, and eventually full-stack development.

    

    

      
Task organization
Plan work, set priorities, and track progress.

      
Team workspaces
Give each team a home for members and projects.

      
Personal to-do
Keep private reminders separate from team responsibilities.

      
Collaboration, with context
Make updates and next steps easier to follow.
